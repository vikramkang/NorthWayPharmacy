Respectful Environment

Everyone who walks through our doors deserves to feel safe and welcome. We do not tolerate abusive language, threats, harassment, or discrimination toward our staff or other patients.

Behaviour of this kind may result in being asked to leave, and in the clinic ending the care relationship.
